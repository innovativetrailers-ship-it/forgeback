## 1. VERDICT
Not launch-ready. Two findings are launch-stoppers regardless of features:
1. **Authentication is client-spoofable.** `src/proxy.ts:39-47` only sets `x-user-id`/`x-user-role` when a session exists; with no session
   it falls through and the client's own headers reach the handlers. 283 of 285 API routes authenticate solely from `x-user-id`;
   admin routes trust `x-user-role`. `admin/promote` (read by me, :6-28) lets anyone send `x-user-role: ADMIN` and grant ADMIN +
   9,999,999 credits. Confirmed by code; not exercised live (the audit sandbox could not reach the site). One-line self-check on
   your Mac: `curl -s -H 'x-user-id: nope' https://forgecinema.vercel.app/api/credits/balance` — if it answers `User not found`
   (404) instead of `Unauthorized` (401), the header is trusted in production.
2. **Billing loses and duplicates money.** No Stripe event idempotency; credit-pack purchases write no ledger row and the nightly
   `reconcileLedgers` then overwrites the balance from the ledger sum (purchased credits wiped); `invoice.paid` overwrites the balance.
   Also ~13 paid routes run model work and never charge (verified 0 hold/deduct references in studio/looks, studio/avatar,
   studio/scene/generate, studio/shot/anchor/generate, studio/compositor/render, etc.) while returning `metered:true`.

Everything else below is real but second order.
